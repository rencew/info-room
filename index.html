How It Works

1️⃣ Sign Up

Create your student account and submit your Certificate of Enrollment for verification.

2️⃣ Get Approved

An administrator will review your registration before you can access the forum.

3️⃣ Join the Forum

Once approved, log in and start sharing and interacting with fellow students.
